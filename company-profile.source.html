01 — Welcome

A decade of keeping Pakistan's telecom hardware online

Al Noor Engineers (Pvt.) Ltd. is a Pakistan-based B2B firm with more than ten years in telecom equipment repair, maintenance and supply — new and refurbished. Our clients are GSM and PSTN operators, telecom vendors and enterprise infrastructure teams. We repair to operator standard, document the work, and stand behind every unit that leaves the lab.

The company operates a designated repair lab equipped for bench and field work: fault isolation, component-level rework, load testing and the documentation that goes out with every repaired unit. On the supply side we stock and source test instruments, fiber tools and telecom hardware with proper part traceability — delivered to site anywhere in Pakistan.
